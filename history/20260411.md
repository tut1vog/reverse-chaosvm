## 28.5: Investigate errorCode -1 ticket validity and errorCode 12 root cause

**Outcome**: investigation complete — findings below

**What was done**: Patched scraper to accept errorCode -1 with ticket as success. Ran scraper 4 times (3 retries each = ~30 verify attempts total). Compiled template → errorCode correlation.

|----------|---------|---------------|-----------|-------|

**Key findings**:
