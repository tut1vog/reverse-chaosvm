**Outcome**: passed

**What was done**: Subagent re-read fn 22317 around pc 23949 and confirmed the sort call site exactly where 44.2.6 reported it: pcs 23885..23894 stage the `[arr,"sort"]` pair, pc 23895 `OP_06 23945` jumps over the closure body, pc 23945 `OP_58 23898 0 0` creates the comparator closure, pc 23949 `OP_02 1` is the `arr.sort(cmp)` METHOD_CALL.
