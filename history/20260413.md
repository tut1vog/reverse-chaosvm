**Notes**: the **biggest finding is a correction to 43.1**: the "constant 2-byte trailer `10 40`" claim was a phantom. Also unexpectedly: the existing `vdata-dynamic-trace.js` encoder is wrong on the trailing 2 chars (re-encoding HAR ciphertext via its `customBase64Encode` produces `...5RsJR4VG` instead of HAR's `...5RsJRiYY`); 43.3 will supersede it.
